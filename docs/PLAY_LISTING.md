## Full description (mobile-adapted; ~2.4k / 4000 chars)

If tracking your health care makes you want to remove your own eye with a spork, but your doctor has threatened to reschedule on you if you show up again with "i unno?" — this app is for you. If you lost your last SSDI appeal because they said you could take a lower-paying job as a Walmart greeter, when we both know Walmart won't accommodate you any better than your last job did — this app is for you. If you need a small explosion of confetti for brushing your teeth in the morning, or literally just surviving — this app is for you.

However, if that confetti risks giving you a migraine or a seizure? We still gotchu — you can turn it all off. This app is STILL for you.

Health care should not be boring, icky, or more exhausting than being alive — and being alive while more broken than most tracking apps assume is exhausting enough. Your data stays 100% private. No ads. No subscriptions. You own the software.

15 themes. 50+ things you can track. And because all that STUFF can be visually overwhelming, you can turn off anything you don't want or need. The reproductive section is gender-neutral: toggle fertility on or off, change the parenting terms, and we make no assumptions about how conception might occur.

Everyone on the same phone can share one install without sharing a database (explicitly allowed with your purchase!) — a simple PIN spins up a whole new database, for those of us primary parents tracking multiple medically interesting children. Encrypted exports and backups. Optional sync between your phone and your desktop (separate purchase).

And the medically interesting photos you save — that rash, that wound, that "is this getting worse?" — are saved INTO the app, not to your phone's camera roll. So you never have to worry about your rash floating up in the family photo album on the TV in the living room.

And PDF exports that speak doctor or lawyer — hand over months of your tracked history with Pearson correlations, without having to remember a thing.

(Two things live on the **desktop** app, not mobile: the no-code custom-tracker builder and lab/medical document uploads — a phone's storage ceiling can't safely hold a document library. So mobile stays fast and focused on daily tracking; the heavy lifting is on desktop.)

There's a full demo of the screens (no working database needed) at https://tryme.chaoscommand.center, because us neurodivergent folks need to click the buttons. Try it; if it turns out not to work on your device for any reason, request a refund — I will not fight you. It's also PolyForm Noncommercial on GitHub for anyone for whom $25 is this week's gas money (valid!). And if compiling from source isn't in your wheelhouse but you'd like to get to work this week — email us. We get a limited number of promo coupons each quarter; we won't make you justify it, just know they're limited.

Thank you for looking. Welcome to Chaos Command.

## ⚠️ Play-policy notes (read before submitting)
1. **Off-platform mentions (GitHub source + "email us for a free key"):** Play's
   payments policy forbids steering users to *alternative ways to PAY* for in-app
   goods. These aren't that — they're *free* (open source + free scholarship keys),
   not an alternate paid channel, so it should pass. (Apple would reject it; Play is
   lenient here.) Low risk, but if a reviewer ever flags it, softening the wording
   is the fix — keep the ethos.
2. **Refund line:** Play processes refunds through Google Play billing (auto within
   48h, plus your discretion in the Console). "I will not fight you" is a lovely
   support promise — just honor it via the Play Console refund flow.
3. **Pricing model must match the copy:** set it up as **free app + one-time
   in-app purchase** ($25 unlock) + a **separate one-time IAP** for sync. Use
   *managed products*, NOT subscriptions (the copy promises "no subscriptions").
4. **Health Apps declaration** (separate form, not this listing): you'll attest
   it's a tracking tool. The local-first / no-data-collection story makes the
   **Data Safety form a layup** — "no data collected, no data shared," and true.
5. **Category:** Medical (or Health & Fitness if Medical gets extra review).
6. **Mobile vs desktop:** no-code tracker builder + lab/medical document upload are
   **disabled on mobile** (≤150MB Dexie ceiling, iOS hits it fast) → removed from
   this listing. **Everything else is the same Tauri app Ren has used daily for
   two months (57 days of survival-checklist data) — PDF export, sync, encrypted
   backups, all 50+ trackers: validated by real daily use.** The listing is
   accurate.
